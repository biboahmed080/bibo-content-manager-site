Support

Need help?

Contact BIBO Content Manager support for connection, privacy, data deletion, or general application questions.

General support

For installation, platform connection, import, analytics, or other product questions, email Marwanmohamed7883@outlook.com.

Privacy & data requests

For access, deletion, or privacy-related questions, use the same support email and include "Privacy Request" in the subject line.

Disconnecting a platform

Use the disconnect option inside BIBO Content Manager. You can also revoke access from the connected platform's account settings where that platform provides the option.

Local data

The current desktop version is local-first. If you need help identifying or removing local application data, contact support and include your Windows version.
