Privacy Policy

This policy explains how BIBO Content Manager handles information when you use the Windows desktop application and this website.

Effective date: August 16, 2026

BIBO Content Manager is a local-first Windows desktop application. The current version does not require a BIBO cloud account for storing your content library or connected-platform credentials.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to BIBO Content Manager (the "App") and the BIBO Content Manager informational website. It describes the information the App may access when you choose to connect supported third-party platforms.

2. Information we handle

Depending on the features you use, the App may handle:

  • Content-library information you create locally, such as titles, notes, production status, content type, target dates, and platform status.
  • Basic connected-account information returned by an authorized platform, such as account identifiers, display name, and profile image when permitted.
  • Public-content metadata you choose to preview or import, such as content identifiers, titles/descriptions, publication dates, thumbnails, duration, dimensions, and public URLs when the platform provides them.
  • Performance metrics made available by an authorized platform, such as views, likes, comments, shares, watch time, or similar metrics when permitted by the granted scopes.
  • OAuth authorization data needed to maintain a connection, such as access tokens, refresh tokens, token expiration information, and granted scopes.

3. Connected-platform data

The App accesses a connected platform only after you initiate and approve that platform's official authorization flow. The exact data available depends on the permissions you approve and the platform's API.

TikTok

When TikTok integration is enabled and you authorize it, the App may request only the TikTok permissions necessary for the features being used, such as basic profile information and access to the user's public video list where approved. The App does not request posting permissions unless a future feature explicitly requires them and you are shown that permission request.

YouTube

When you authorize YouTube, the App may access permitted account, public-content, and analytics information necessary for the connection, import, and analytics features you choose to use.

4. Local storage and security

In the current desktop version, application records are stored locally on the user's Windows device. OAuth credentials are stored locally using the App's encrypted credential vault. The App does not intentionally publish your OAuth tokens or local content database to this public website.

No method of storage is completely risk-free. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your Windows account and device.

5. How information is used

Information handled by the App is used to provide features you request, including:

  • Connecting and identifying authorized social accounts.
  • Displaying and syncing permitted content metadata.
  • Previewing and importing authorized public content into your local content library.
  • Displaying analytics and performance information provided by supported APIs.
  • Maintaining connection state and refreshing authorization tokens where supported.
  • Preventing duplicate imports and supporting disconnect/reconnect workflows.

6. Sharing and sale of information

BIBO Content Manager does not sell your personal information. The App communicates with the third-party platform you choose to connect in order to perform the API requests you authorize. Those third parties handle information under their own privacy policies and terms.

We may disclose information if required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect the security and integrity of the App and its users.

7. Retention and deletion

Local application data generally remains on your device until you delete the relevant records, disconnect an account, clear the App's local data, or otherwise remove the corresponding local files. Disconnecting a platform is designed to remove the App's active connection credentials for that platform; where supported, the App may also request revocation from the platform.

If you need help identifying or deleting BIBO Content Manager data on your device, contact us at Marwanmohamed7883@outlook.com.

8. Your choices and requests

You can choose whether to connect a supported platform. You can also:

  • Disconnect a connected platform from the App.
  • Revoke the App's authorization from the connected platform's own account settings where available.
  • Delete content records you created or imported into the local content library.
  • Request support for access, correction, or deletion questions by emailing us.

9. Children

BIBO Content Manager is a creator productivity application and is not directed to children. Users must comply with the age requirements of the third-party platforms they choose to connect.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App changes, new integrations are added, or legal and platform requirements evolve. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material revisions are published.
